Privacy policy.

Last updated: January 2026

Whisk & Timber Artisan Bakery (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or contact us, how we use that information, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us through our contact form, such as your name, email address, phone number, event date, guest count, and any details about your event or order. We only collect what you choose to share with us.

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party analytics tools to monitor your behavior across the web.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your inquiry, prepare a quote, plan your event or order, and communicate with you about your request. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses Formspree to process contact form submissions. When you submit a form, your information is transmitted through Formspree’s service and delivered to us via email. Formspree has its own privacy practices, which you can review on their website.

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Standard server logs may record general visit information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited. These logs are not used to identify individual visitors.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable precautions to protect the information you share with us. Form submissions are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and we limit access to your information to only those who need it to fulfill your request.

Your Choices

You may request that we delete the contact information you have shared with us at any time. To make a request, please call us at (903) 339-1009 or email whiskandtimberbakery@gmail.com. We will honor your request within a reasonable time frame, except where retention is required by law.

Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the website after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Call us at (903) 339-1009, email whiskandtimberbakery@gmail.com, or visit us at 1828 South Jackson Street, Jacksonville, TX 75766.

Accessibility statement.

Last updated: January 2026

Whisk & Timber Artisan Bakery is committed to making our website welcoming and accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We believe everyone should be able to learn about our bakery, plan their event, and reach out to us with ease.

Our Commitment

We work to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at the AA level, the widely recognized standard for web accessibility. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we continue to review and improve the site over time.

What We’ve Done

Our site is built to be navigable by keyboard and compatible with screen readers. We provide descriptive alt text for images, clear page structure with proper headings, readable color contrast, and a layout that adapts to phones, tablets, and desktops.

Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some content may not be fully accessible to every visitor. If you encounter a barrier or have trouble using any part of the site, we want to hear from you so we can address it.

Need Help or Have Feedback?

If you have difficulty accessing any content on our website, or if you have suggestions for making it better, please call us at (903) 339-1009 or email whiskandtimberbakery@gmail.com. We’re happy to assist you directly, take your order over the phone, or provide information in another format.
